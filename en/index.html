Small, quiet, beautiful

Backroundo lives as a small icon in the menu bar. One click shows the current image, where it comes from and what you can do next.

Changes on its own

Every 15 minutes, hourly or once a day – or only when you click “Next Wallpaper”.

Smooth transition

The old image fades into the new one in about a second instead of jumping.

Favorites

Click the heart and the image is saved to your favorites folder in “Pictures”.

Multiple displays

The same image everywhere or a different one on each display.

Starts with your Mac

If you like, Backroundo runs in the background from the moment you log in.

No data, no ads

No account, no tracking, no statistics. Everything stays on your Mac.

Four image sources

Tick the ones you like. With several sources, Backroundo picks one at random each time. Wikimedia is already on, so the app works right away.

Installation

Takes a minute. The detailed manual (PDF) is also included in the download.

1

Open the DMG

Double-click Backroundo-1.2.dmg in your Downloads folder.

2

Drag to Applications

Drag Backroundo onto the Applications shortcut, then eject the drive.

3

Launch

Open Backroundo from Applications. Its icon appears at the top right of the menu bar.

“Backroundo can’t be opened”?

Backroundo is free and not registered with Apple as a developer app, so macOS blocks the first launch. Here is how to open it anyway – only once:

  1. Click Done in the message.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down.
  3. Next to “Backroundo was blocked”, click Open Anyway and enter your password.

Or enter this once in the Terminal app:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Backroundo.app
